## Executive Summary

v3.0 delivered significant AI infrastructure improvements and a modern chat experience, but **3 of 17 requirements (TRIAGE-01/02/03) were never implemented** — Phase 03 (Auto-Triage Core) was never planned or built. Additionally, **6 of 11 phases lack formal verification**, and the SnoozeDialog UI gap from Phase 04 remains open.

The core value proposition of "intelligent email management" is partially delivered through triage field exposure (Phase 08) and tool rendering (Phase 09), but without the auto-triage engine (Phase 03), the data pipeline has no source.

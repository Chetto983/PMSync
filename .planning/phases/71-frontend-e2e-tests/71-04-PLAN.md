**Step 1 — Legal consent:**
- Navigate to `/onboarding`
- Find the TOS acceptance toggle/checkbox and enable it
- Find the privacy policy toggle and enable it
- Find the AI consent toggle and enable it (or decline — depends on wizard logic)
- Click "Continue" or "Next"

**Step 2 — Name confirm:**
- The name field should be pre-filled with the registered user's name
- Optionally update it
- Click "Continue"

**Step 3 — Workspace:**
- Click "Create workspace" or fill in workspace name
- Submit
- Wait for success and "Continue"

**Step 4 — Email provider:**
- Click "Skip for now" button
- Per research and CONTEXT.md decision: no OAuth mock, just skip

**Step 5 — Avatar:**
- Click "Skip" button (or equivalent — confirmed in OnboardingWizard.tsx)

**Step 6 — MFA setup:**
- Set up TOTP intercept BEFORE clicking "Enable TOTP":
  ```typescript
  let totpUri = '';
  await page.route('**/two-factor/enable', async (route) => {
    const response = await route.fetch();
    const body = await response.json();
    totpUri = body.totpURI ?? '';
    await route.fulfill({ response });
  });
  ```
- Click "Set up authenticator app" or "Enable TOTP" option
- If prompted for password, fill `ONBOARDING_USER_PASSWORD`
- Click "Continue"
- After page.route intercepts the response, `totpUri` is populated
- Extract secret: `const secret = new URL(totpUri).searchParams.get('secret')!`
- Generate code immediately: `const code = authenticator.generate(secret)`
- Fill the OTP input: `page.fill('[inputmode="numeric"]', code)`
- Click "Verify" or "Enable"
- Wait for success step

  </behavior>
  <action>

    4. After the replacement path is proven by tests, enable `account.encryptOAuthTokens: true` in the Better Auth config.
    5. Remove any remaining PMSync reads that require plaintext `account.accessToken` or `account.refreshToken`.
  </action>

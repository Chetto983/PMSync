## PII Resolution Flow (Important)
1. User says "Send email to john@example.com" in chat
2. `PiiDetectionService.detect()` redacts to `[EMAIL_REDACTED:abc123]`
3. `PiiResolutionService.storeMapping()` saves mapping in Redis with `sessionId` key
4. LLM generates SEND_EMAIL action with redacted placeholder
5. HITL stores action with `sessionId` in `actionPayload`
6. On approval, `ActionExecutorService.execute()` calls `resolvePayload()` with correct `sessionId`
7. Email is sent with actual email address restored

The bug was at step 5 - we were storing `executionId` instead of `sessionId`, breaking step 6.
</context>
